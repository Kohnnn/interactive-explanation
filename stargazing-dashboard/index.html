Observation deck

A neutral sky lab for time, place, and visibility.

Set a position, move time, soften the sky with weather, then watch the HUD describe the current local dome. Drag the canvas or use the arrow keys to aim, and the rendered stars, constellations, sun, moon, and planets update in real time as place and time change.
