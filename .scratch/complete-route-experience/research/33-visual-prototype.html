Essay / notice the uncertainty

A tidy result is not a settled result.

Imagine drawing a handful of counters from a bag. Seeing mostly blue counters tells you what you drew, not exactly what remains inside. Another handful could tell a different story.

The useful question is not “Does this picture look convincing?” It is “What would change my mind?” Name the missing information before treating a pattern as a rule.

What this analogy leaves out

Real observations may depend on each other or come from a biased selection. A larger sample alone does not remove those problems. The control below is a layout demonstration, not a statistical calculation.
