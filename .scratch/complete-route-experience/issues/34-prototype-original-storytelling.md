## Question

Which original editorial voice and interaction-led story structure make these subjects clearer without flattening their distinct learning experiences?

## Decision boundaries

- Build a small disposable comparison using representative essay, lab, and practice experiences selected from baseline evidence and the approved visual direction.
- Explore an original voice, meaningful questions, explanatory pacing, feedback, and learner agency rather than reskinning inherited prose or copying the sibling product's racing narrative.
- Treat predict/manipulate/observe/explain/transfer as a candidate sequence, not a predetermined template or a requirement for every Route.
- Decide when interaction teaches something and when a concise explanation, diagram, or existing runtime is the better choice.
- Preserve factual accuracy, sources, licensing, attribution, and local provenance; distinguish new authored interpretation from retained source material and illustrative claims.
- Respect the reconciled behavior/content boundaries and existing URLs, user data, accessibility, and runtime seams.
- Review keyboard/touch equivalence, mobile reading order, feedback states, reset/recovery, and compatibility with the approved light/dark direction.
- Describe expected learning value as a hypothesis. Do not claim measured learning improvement without actual learner evidence.

## Answer

Select **intent-shaped question, action, consequence, reason, and optional transfer** under the user's autopilot delegation. This is agent editorial selection, not human approval or evidence of improved learning. [Storytelling decision report](../research/34-storytelling-prototype.md) records the complete lesson/state contracts, alternatives, accuracy checks, and provenance; [disposable artifact](../research/34-storytelling-prototype.html) and [runnable checks](../research/34-storytelling-prototype.mjs) demonstrate the decision.

- Essay: orient with a concrete question and stated assumptions, compare an observation, then interpret. The fixed-target toy computes squared bias and empirical variance with equal MSE for two different prediction sets; it does not claim a universal monotonic complexity tradeoff or generalization proof.
- Lab: lead with manipulation, show relevant dependency state, explain an invalid operation, and allow immediate recovery. The receipt example explicitly models non-cryptographic link state; it neither replaces the real engine nor claims mining, consensus, authenticity, or security.
- Practice: use a short prompt, immediate recoverable explanatory feedback, and optional hints. The D-major exercise teaches successive letter spelling and semitone pattern without pretending to supply audio or persistent Progress.
- Preserve native keyboard/touch operations, visible textual state, independent reset, and original authorship records. Do not force a five-step wizard, an essay before practice, decorative controls, or a new shared lesson runtime.
- Verification: inline syntax passes, 12/12 pure-check groups pass, and 6/6 viewport/theme cells pass with zero overflow and tested controls at least 44px. Maths, guarded repair order, retry/reset, theme changes, emulated tap and keyboard paths, and token contrast were checked. Production integration, real users/devices, field performance, and full accessibility coverage remain unmeasured.
