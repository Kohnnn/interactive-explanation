Why this lesson matters

This is the family bridge from isolated drills into arrangement thinking. It makes rhythm, bass motion, harmony, and melodic shape audible as one system.

Shared loop: all four tracks ride one transport — play or stop in any track drives the whole loop. Listen: start with the beat before changing any pitched track. Edit: move one layer at a time so the effect stays audible. Loop: let the transport keep running while you compare sections.
