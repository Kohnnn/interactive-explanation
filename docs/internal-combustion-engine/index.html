Source snapshot

The local route vendors the published article HTML, shared article CSS, page-local CSS, route-local engine model data, and scripts, then patches only the subpath-hosting shell and provenance surfaces needed for the public replica.

  • Live source snapshot verified and archived for this route on 2026-03-21.
  • The local copy removes the upstream banner, blog navigation, analytics, Patreon prompt, and social links from the public body.
  • The shipped route now adds a compact engineering-sandbox hero and reinforced generated chapter navigation while leaving the article runtime in place.
  • The crank, block, piston, head, valve, camshaft, pressure, flywheel, and starter sections remain driven by the vendored scripts rather than a local rewrite; a linked four-stroke recap now accompanies the cycle scene.

Asset handoff

  • internal-combustion-engine/index.html owns the local metadata shell, shared footer hook, and relative article asset paths.
  • internal-combustion-engine/css/base.css and internal-combustion-engine/css/ice.css preserve the published article layout and interaction styling with subpath-safe helper image URLs.
  • internal-combustion-engine/js/base.js and internal-combustion-engine/js/ice.js keep the original drag, slider, and animation runtime.
  • internal-combustion-engine/models/ contains the route-local geometry blobs requested by the published engine scenes.

Known deviations

  • The public route no longer includes the author banner, Patreon prompt, X/Twitter, Instagram, e-mail, RSS, or Google Analytics surfaces.
  • The local copy uses local fallback font stacks instead of runtime Google Fonts requests so the route stays self-contained.
  • The onboarding shell is additive: it introduces local hero framing and reinforced chapter navigation without rewriting the underlying article scenes.
  • In-body longform engineering article links are rewritten locally when available or neutralized when no local replica exists yet.

Validation guidance

  • Keep the article structure intact; future edits should patch the published shell, not rewrite the interactions into a condensed local experience.
  • Extend the shared smoke bundle through the engineering-longform family helpers instead of creating a one-off validator.
  • Run both node interactive-explanation/tools/check-public-surface.mjs and node interactive-explanation/tools/smoke-bundle.mjs after touching article shell copy or any vendored runtime files.
